# How to Convert Images Without Losing Quality

## Hero Introduction
Converting an image from one format to another (such as PNG to JPG or vice versa) shouldn't result in blurry text or distorted colors. Depending on the target format, files are processed using lossy or lossless compression. This guide outlines how to convert images without losing quality, processing everything safely in your browser.

## Quick Answer
To convert images without losing quality, choose a lossless format like PNG or WebP Lossless. If you must convert to a lossy format like JPEG, set the export quality factor to at least **90%** to prevent compression artifacts.

## The Science of Conversion Loss
When you convert an image:
- **Lossy to Lossy (e.g. JPG to JPG)**: Every save operation recalculates block colors, adding artifacts.
- **Lossless to Lossy (e.g. PNG to JPG)**: Discards high-frequency variations to shrink file size.
- **Lossy to Lossless (e.g. JPG to PNG)**: Preserves the file exactly as it is, but cannot recover details already lost in the original.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- **Converting Low-Res Files to Lossless**: Saving a blurry JPG as a PNG will only increase the file size, not improve its quality.
- **Losing Original Color Spaces**: Make sure your converter respects embedded sRGB color profiles to prevent colors from shifting.
